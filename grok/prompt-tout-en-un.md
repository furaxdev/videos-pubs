# Prompt Grok — TOUT EN UN (planche de 12 poses du même personnage)

1. Dans Grok, joins l'image de ton avatar (ta photo de profil dessinée).
2. Colle le prompt ci-dessous en entier (en anglais, Grok le comprend mieux).

---

Use the attached image as the character reference. Create ONE single image: a character sheet laid out as a clean grid of 4 columns x 3 rows (12 equal cells, no overlap, a little space between cells). Every cell contains the EXACT same character: same face, same hairstyle, same skin tone, same glasses/beard if any, same body proportions, wearing a black t-shirt with a small orange "</>" logo on the chest, dark blue jeans and white sneakers.

Style for ALL cells: flat 2D cartoon illustration, bold clean dark outlines, soft cel shading, vibrant colors, bitmoji-style full-body character, thick white sticker border around the character, centered in its cell, full body visible from head to shoes, plain pure white background (#FFFFFF), no text, no labels, no numbers, no ground shadow, no extra objects except the ones listed. Same lighting and same drawing style in every cell. Do not change the character between cells.

Poses and expressions, left to right, top to bottom:
1. Waving with the right hand raised, big friendly smile, looking at the viewer.
2. Arms crossed, confident smirk, one eyebrow raised.
3. Pointing with the right index finger to the side, smiling, body slightly turned.
4. Holding an open laptop in one arm, the other hand typing, the laptop screen showing an orange "</>" symbol, focused smile.
5. Both hands on the cheeks, mouth wide open, big surprised eyes (shocked).
6. Both arms raised high in victory, mouth open in joy, jumping slightly.
7. Shrugging, palms up, eyebrows raised, small awkward smile.
8. Thumbs up with one hand, winking, big smile.
9. Peace sign with the right hand, tongue slightly out, happy.
10. Angry: furrowed eyebrows, fists clenched, steam coming from the head.
11. Sad: head down, shoulders slumped, small frown.
12. Crying: eyes closed, blue tears streaming down, mouth open, hands wiping the eyes.

High resolution, wide 4:3 landscape image.

---

## Si ça ne marche pas du premier coup
- Le visage change d'une case à l'autre : ajoute « Keep the exact same face and hairstyle in all 12 cells, copy them from the reference image. » et régénère.
- Des cases sont fusionnées ou coupées : ajoute « Each cell must be separated by clear white space. Do not crop any character. »
- Tu veux une pose seule, en grand (pour la vidéo) : envoie à Grok, avec la planche jointe :
  « Output only cell number 6 as a single full-body image, same character, same style, plain white background, 3:4 vertical, high resolution. »
- Ensuite, enlève le fond blanc (remove.bg, Canva…) pour avoir un PNG transparent avec le contour blanc.

## Les autres visuels de la vidéo (à part, ils ne sont pas dans la planche)
- Image de chat : « Ultra cute ginger tabby kitten sitting and looking straight at the camera, big green eyes, soft studio lighting, shallow depth of field, pastel peach background, photorealistic, 4:5 vertical. »
- Mascotte Claude Code : utilise le fichier officiel `brand/assets/clawd-claude-code.png` plutôt qu'une version générée.
