# Prompt Gemini — vrai MANGA en couleurs (pas cute)

Joins la planche de Grok (12 poses) + ton avatar d'origine, puis colle :

---

Redraw the attached character sheet as a real, serious FULL-COLOR MANGA in the style of modern shonen / seinen anime (think professional manga illustration, NOT cute, NOT chibi, NOT kawaii). Keep the exact same character identity, the same layout (grid of 4 columns x 3 rows, 12 equal cells), the same 12 poses and expressions in the same order, the same clothes (black t-shirt with a small orange "</>" logo, dark blue jeans, white sneakers), and the same face, hairstyle and skin tone.

Style rules:
- Realistic, athletic manga proportions (about 7.5 heads tall), long legs, defined shoulders. No big head, no round baby face.
- Sharp, confident black ink linework with strong variation in line weight (thick outer contour, thin inner details), hand-inked look.
- Eyes: narrower and sharper than cute anime, detailed iris with highlights, strong eyebrows, intense expressive look. Small nose, defined jaw, angular face.
- Hair: dark, messy, drawn as sharp clumps and spiky strands with white highlights.
- Full color with cel shading: crisp hard-edged two-tone shadows, rich saturated colors, clean highlights on cloth folds, subtle halftone (screentone) dots inside the shadows, slight rim light.
- Dynamic, dramatic manga expressions: determined, fierce, shocked with shock lines, angry with a vein mark and steam, sad with shadowed eyes, crying with realistic tear trails. Serious tone, no sparkles, no hearts, no cute blush.

Keep: thick white sticker outline around each character, full body visible head to shoes, plain pure white background (#FFFFFF) in every cell, no text, no labels, no numbers, no ground shadow, no background scenery. Same drawing style and lighting in all 12 cells. High resolution, wide 4:3 landscape image.
