# Prompt Gemini — version MANGA de la planche de 12 poses

1. Dans Gemini, joins la planche générée par Grok (12 poses) ET, si tu l'as, ton avatar d'origine.
2. Colle le prompt ci-dessous.

---

Redraw the attached character sheet in a MANGA / ANIME style. Keep the exact same character identity, the same layout (grid of 4 columns x 3 rows, 12 equal cells), the same 12 poses and expressions in the same order, the same clothes (black t-shirt with a small orange "</>" logo, dark blue jeans, white sneakers), and the same face, hairstyle and skin tone, simply translated into manga style.

Manga style: Japanese shonen anime look, clean confident ink linework with varied line weight, large expressive eyes with highlights, sharp stylized hair strands, simplified nose and mouth, slightly dynamic proportions, cel shading with crisp two-tone shadows, vivid saturated colors, subtle halftone/screentone texture in the shadows. Expressions exaggerated the manga way: sweat drop for awkward, anger vein and steam for angry, sparkle eyes for joy, comic tears for crying, shock lines for surprise.

Keep: thick white sticker outline around each character, full body visible head to shoes, plain pure white background (#FFFFFF) in every cell, no text, no labels, no numbers, no ground shadow, no background scenery. Same drawing style and lighting in all 12 cells. High resolution, wide 4:3 landscape image.

---

## Variantes
- Noir et blanc (vrai manga papier) : remplace « vivid saturated colors » par « black and white manga, screentone shading, strong ink blacks, no color except the orange "</>" logo ».
- Plus "chibi" (mignon) : ajoute « chibi proportions, big head, small body, cute ».
- Plus "seinen" (réaliste) : ajoute « realistic proportions, mature seinen manga style, detailed shading ».

## Si ça ne marche pas du premier coup
- Le visage ressemble moins : ajoute « Keep the face recognisable: same eye shape, eyebrows, hair parting and face shape as the reference. »
- Une pose est différente : ajoute « Do not change any pose, only the art style. »
- Une seule pose en grand : « Output only cell number 6 as a single full-body image, same style, plain white background, 3:4 vertical, high resolution. »
